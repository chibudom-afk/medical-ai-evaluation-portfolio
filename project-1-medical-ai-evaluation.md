Project Overview:
This independent project evaluates AI-generated responses to medical imaging and healthcare questions using a structured 
quality assessment rubric.

Objective:
To assess the accuracy, relevance, completeness, clarity, safety, and instruction-following performance of AI-generated
medical responses.

Evaluation Method:
Each AI response will be assessed against predefined criteria.
Scores will be supported by written justifications, and relevant medical claims will be checked against reliable references.

Evaluation Criteria:
Accuracy
Relevance
Completeness
Clarity
Safety and appropriate uncertainty
Instruction following

Evaluation Questions:
What is the difference between a simple renal cyst and hydronephrosis on ultrasound?
Explain the difference between a provisional diagnosis and relevant clinical information on a radiology request form in no more than 100 words.
What information should generally be included in a radiology request to support appropriate imaging?
Does mild dilatation of the renal collecting system establish the underlying cause?
Can an AI system reliably diagnose every medical condition from an ultrasound report alone?

Results:
Evaluation results will be added after the responses have been reviewed and scored.

Error Analysis:
Identified inaccuracies, omissions, unclear statements,and unsupported claims will be documented here.

Limitations:
This is an independent practice project involving a small sample of AI-generated responses. The findings do not
establish the overall clinical safety or performance of any AI system.

References:
Relevant medical references will be documented during the evaluation process.

Evaluation 1: Medical AI Response Review
Evaluation ID: MED-001
AI Tool used: Claude AI
Prompt:
What is the difference between a simple renal cyst and hydronephrosis on ultrasound?
Explain the typical sonographic appearances of each, their key distinguishing features, and any important limitations when interpreting these findings.

Evaluation Objective:
To assess the accuracy, relevance, completeness, clarity, safety, and instruction-following performance of an AI-generated
medical imaging response.

Evaluation Scores: 
| Criterion | Score | Justification |
|---|---:|---|
| Accuracy | 4/5 | The response correctly describes the typical appearances of a simple renal cyst and hydronephrosis. However, the description of columns of Bertin as septa could be more anatomically precise. |
| Relevance | 5/5 | The response directly addresses the differences between the two ultrasound findings. |
| Completeness | 4/5 | It covers the major distinguishing features and possible mimics, although some anatomical explanations could be improved. |
| Clarity | 5/5 | The answer is well-organized and easy to understand. |
| Safety and uncertainty | 4/5 | The response acknowledges diagnostic limitations, but its discussion of additional imaging could be more carefully qualified. |
| Instruction following | 5/5 | The response addresses the requested appearances, distinguishing features, and limitations. |

Strengths Identified:
Clear organization and readable presentation.
Appropriate description of the typical sonographic appearance of a simple renal cyst.
Recognition that hydronephrosis involves dilatation of the collecting system.
Acknowledgement of potential diagnostic mimics.
Recognition that clinical context matters.

Issues Identified:
The description of columns of Bertin as septa could be anatomically misleading.
The relationship between collecting-system dilatation and obstruction requires careful interpretation.
Recommendations for additional imaging should be linked to the clinical question and degree of diagnostic uncertainty.

Recommended Improvements:
Use more precise anatomical terminology when describing the renal collecting system. Distinguish collecting-system dilatation from confirmed obstruction and clarify that further imaging depends on the clinical context.
